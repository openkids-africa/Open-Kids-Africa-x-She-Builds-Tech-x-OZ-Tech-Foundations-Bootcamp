# Open Kids Africa × She Builds Tech × OZ — Tech Foundations Bootcamp

> **A free, mentor-led 10-week technology education program for Kids in Africa.**
> Powered by Open Kids Africa, She Builds Tech, and the Oz community.

---

## About the Program

The **Tech Foundations Bootcamp** exists because access — not talent — is the biggest barrier standing between young African Kids and careers in technology.

This program is a structured, cohort-based initiative that brings together three organisations with a shared mission: to give Kids their first real, hands-on experience with coding and digital technology, in a safe, supportive, and mentor-led environment — completely free of charge.

Cohort 1 is our pilot. It is deliberately small, carefully designed, and built to prove that this model works, so that we can scale it, replicate it, and reach more kids across Africa in future cohorts.

---

## The Partnership

This program is made possible by three organisations working together across their areas of strength.

### Open Kids Africa
Open Kids Africa leads on everything that happens on the ground. This includes identifying and confirming the pilot school, managing student enrollment, engaging with families and the local community, coordinating session logistics, and ensuring the program is delivered in a way that is appropriate and accessible for the school context. Open Kids Africa is also responsible for the student assessment process and the production of the Cohort 1 Impact Report.

### She Builds Tech
She Builds Tech leads on the curriculum, the mentor community, and the technical delivery of the program. She Builds Tech recruits and trains all mentors, facilitates the joint training workshop, manages the weekly mentor debrief calls, and oversees the Oz platform environment from setup through to completion.

### Oz Platform
The Oz platform is the digital learning environment that powers every session of the bootcamp. It provides the interactive coding and technology curriculum that students work through during each session. Oz is configured specifically for this cohort — with accounts, learning pathways, and session content set up and tested before the program begins.

Together, these three organisations cover the full delivery chain: community and school access (Open Kids Africa), professional mentorship and curriculum (She Builds Tech), and technology infrastructure (Oz).

## Program Structure

The bootcamp runs over 10 weeks with 2 sessions per week, for a total of 20 sessions. Each session is facilitated by a trained mentor and delivered using the Oz platform.

### Pre-Launch Phase (May 4 – June 14, 2026)
All planning, setup, and preparation work happens in the 7 weeks before the cohort launches. This includes program plan approval, school confirmation, student enrollment, mentor recruitment and training, curriculum finalisation, device procurement, and the student pre-assessment.

### Live Cohort Phase (June 15 – August 24, 2026)
The 10-week program runs with 2 sessions per week. Mentors facilitate each session and participate in weekly debrief calls. A mid-cohort check-in is conducted at Week 5 to assess student progress and provide sponsors with a progress update.

### Post-Cohort Phase (September 2026)
Students complete a post-assessment that mirrors the baseline pre-assessment. Results are compiled alongside attendance data, mentor reflections, and session logs into the Cohort 1 Impact Report. A retrospective is held and Cohort 2 planning begins.

## What Students Experience

Students who complete the Tech Foundations Bootcamp go through a full, structured learning journey — not a one-off workshop. Over 10 weeks they:

- Are introduced to coding and digital technology through the Oz platform in a hands-on, project-based environment
- Work alongside professional mentors from the She Builds Tech community who guide, support, and inspire them throughout
- Complete a structured curriculum that builds progressively across 20 sessions
- Track their own growth through a pre-assessment at the start and a post-assessment at the end
- Present their work publicly at Demo Day / Graduation to an audience of parents, sponsors, and community members
- Receive a certificate of completion and become part of the Open Kids Africa and She Builds Tech alumni community

No prior coding experience is required. All devices and materials are provided. The program is 100% free.

---

## Mentors

Mentors are recruited from the She Builds Tech professional community — women working in technology, engineering, product, data, and adjacent fields who commit their time to facilitating sessions and supporting students throughout the cohort.

All mentors complete a joint training workshop delivered by Open Kids Africa and She Builds Tech before the cohort begins. They receive the full curriculum, session guides, and access to the Oz platform. During the cohort, mentors join a weekly debrief call to share feedback, flag issues, and align on the week ahead.

A minimum of 6 mentors are confirmed for Cohort 1.
